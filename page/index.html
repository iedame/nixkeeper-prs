Open issues, by title (their text isn't read). A package's issue is linked to the open PRs for that package. A build failure is checked against what Hydra builds now on master (its platforms, when the title names them): one that builds is a candidate to close, unless the title adds a condition (an option, an override, a setup) only a person can check. With no Hydra job by the title's name, it's checked under the name it has now (an older Python's set in the current one, a name without its version, a rename), or said why there's none: removed from nixpkgs (with nixpkgs' reason, a candidate to close), unfree, marked broken, or no Hydra platforms (Hydra doesn't build those). An update request is checked against the version nixpkgs has now (master's, else the channel's), by Nix's own order, and against the update bot's queue (what it would update to next, from Repology: now and then another project's version).
